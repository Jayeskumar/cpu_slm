# One plain-English statement per line, in the same controlled English the mind
# understands in conversation. Load it with:
#   python3 -m mnemo feed --pack general
# It complements WordNet: WordNet gives the dictionary (what kind of thing
# everything is); this pack gives common-sense facts, exceptions, and up-to-date
# capitals and currencies. Pack facts about capitals and currencies replace
# WordNet's older ones (WordNet 3.0 dates from 2006).
# Disputed capitals and city-states named like their country are left out.
